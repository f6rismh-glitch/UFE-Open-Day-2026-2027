The Experience

About the Open Day

A full-day experience designed to help you explore UFE, discover your future major, and understand what student life could look like.

More than a visit

A structured day for smarter decisions.

UFE Open Day is designed for prospective students and families who want to explore the French University in Egypt before applying. Visitors will meet faculty, discover majors, explore the future campus through a 3D interactive experience, take a personality-based major quiz, and receive admissions guidance.
