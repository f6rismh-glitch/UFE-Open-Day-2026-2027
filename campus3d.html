Campus Vision

The new campus design was presented by Jakob + MacFarlane for the Université Française d’Égypte in Cairo. Positioned as an innovation-focused campus in El Shorouk, the concept includes academic buildings, research spaces, a learning center, auditoriums, student accommodation, restaurants, administrative spaces, green areas, and parking.

Sustainability Ideas

  • Solar shading and energy-conscious design
  • Reuse of greywater for irrigation
  • Gardens and climate-responsive outdoor spaces
  • Natural ventilation inspired by Egyptian architectural principles
  • Shaded walkways and green campus areas
